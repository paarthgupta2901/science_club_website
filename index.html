About Our Club

The ABC School Science Club is a place where students learn through experiments, activities and projects. Our goal is to develop curiosity and encourage students to discover how the world works.

Students learn about topics such as H2O, CO2, electricity, space and renewable energy. We also conduct experiments related to E=mc2.

New members are welcome.
